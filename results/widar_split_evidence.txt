==============================================================================
EVIDENCE 4: what a pair of recordings has in common, measured on raw data
==============================================================================
Mean cosine similarity between two BVP samples, with no model involved.

the pair shares                             pairs   cosine      sd
same recording condition                     1500    0.565   0.074
same gesture, different condition            1500    0.449   0.071
different gesture                            1385    0.437   0.072

Sharing a gesture is worth +0.012 in similarity. Sharing a recording
condition is worth +0.129, which is 10.5 times more. Two recordings
resemble each other far more for coming from the same person standing in the
same place than for being the same gesture. A model rewarded for accuracy on a
condition-mixed split is therefore rewarded for recognising the condition.

EVIDENCE 5: the benchmark's split can be answered by lookup, with no learning
==============================================================================
Each test sample takes the gesture label of the single most similar training
sample. No training, no parameters, raw BVP only.

protocol            arm                  classes  chance  1-NN acc  mean sim
cross-orientation   cross                     22   0.045     0.101     0.629
                    in_domain                 22   0.045     0.306     0.650
                    in_domain_grouped         22   0.045     0.231     0.644
cross-position      cross                     22   0.045     0.265     0.652
                    in_domain                 22   0.045     0.313     0.651
                    in_domain_grouped         22   0.045     0.233     0.648
cross-user          cross                      9   0.111     0.250     0.651
                    in_domain                  9   0.111     0.381     0.657
                    in_domain_grouped          9   0.111     0.328     0.650

The gap between the two in-domain arms is pure near-duplicate retrieval, because
both are domain-mixed and differ only in whether repeat takes of one recording
condition were allowed to straddle the split. The mean nearest-neighbour
similarity never reaches 1.0, so these are sibling recordings rather than
copies of the same file.
